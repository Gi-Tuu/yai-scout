# ADR Template

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded
- Date:
- Owners:

## Context

What is the situation and which constraints apply?

## Problem

What decision is needed, and why now?

## Considered options

1. Option A — pros / cons
2. Option B — pros / cons

## Decision

What was chosen, and what is explicitly out of scope?

## Consequences

Positive, negative and follow-up effects.

## Migration plan

How do existing data, APIs and deployments move to the new state? What is not migrated?

## Rollback plan

How to revert safely; which changes are additive and can remain?

## Verification

Commands, tests, hashes or smoke checks that prove the decision works.
